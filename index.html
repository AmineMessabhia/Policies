5 Seconds Rule is a family-friendly party trivia game. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information.

1. No account, no sign-up

5 Seconds Rule has no accounts, no sign-up, and no social features. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, or any other personal details inside the app.

The only time we receive any personal information is if you choose to email us first at mrbeardroid@gmail.com — in that case we see whatever you include in your email, and we use it only to reply to you.

2. Information stored on your device (local only)

Everything you type or create in the app is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Player first names typed during game setup are kept only for the current game session. They are not saved after the app closes.
  • Player-authored questions (an optional feature) are stored in a local database on your device. There is no cloud sync — custom questions never leave your device.
  • Settings such as theme and sound preference are stored on your device.
  • Scores are calculated locally and are not transmitted anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes all of this local data.

3. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The app uses two Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous usage statistics (such as which screens are used) so we can understand what is working and improve the game. The app explicitly disables Advertising ID collection for analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash diagnostics (device model, operating system version, and stack traces) so we can find and fix bugs.

This data is processed by Google as our service provider. It is not used to identify you, and we do not combine it with any personal information.

See Google’s privacy policy and Firebase’s privacy information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads and is configured to be family-safe:

  • Banner ads appear on the menu and store screens.
  • Rewarded ads are strictly optional. The app may offer “watch an ad to preview a pack” — you choose whether to watch; nothing plays automatically.
  • No personalized ads. Ads are served as non-personalized. AdMob may still process your device’s advertising ID for non-personalized ad delivery and fraud prevention.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

When purchases are enabled in the app (for example, the ad-free unlock), payments are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees or stores your payment details — card numbers, billing addresses, and similar information stay with Google.

6. Answer search (in-app Google search)

When you tap an answer during a game to look it up, the app opens a Google web search for that answer inside the app (an in-app browser view). The text of that search — the answer you tapped, together with its question — and standard web request data (such as your IP address and basic device/browser information) are sent to Google, which handles it under Google’s own privacy policy.

This happens only when you choose to look an answer up; with that action, nothing else about the game leaves your device.

7. What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with anyone beyond the Google services described above (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play Billing, and the in-app Google search when you choose to use it), which process it under Google’s own privacy policies.
  • We do not run our own servers, and we do not maintain any database of app users.

8. Children’s privacy

5 Seconds Rule is designed to be family-friendly. The app has no account system and does not knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone. Player first names entered during a game are kept only in the current session on the device and are never transmitted.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions or concerns, please contact us at mrbeardroid@gmail.com.

9. Your choices and controls

  • Remove ads: when available, purchasing the ad-free unlock turns off ads.
  • Rewarded ads: simply don’t choose to watch them — they are always opt-in.
  • Device ad settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, and manage ad personalization preferences there.
  • Local data: uninstalling the app removes all data the app stored on your device (game-session names, custom questions, and settings).
  • Questions: email us at mrbeardroid@gmail.com.

10. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data on any server of our own — there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Analytics, crash, ad, and in-app search data are retained and processed by Google LLC under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

All data stored locally by the app is removed when you uninstall the app.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if the app gains new features that change how data is handled. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or the app’s data practices? Email: mrbeardroid@gmail.com
